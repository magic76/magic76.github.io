GOOD TO KNOW / 03

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to bring my own story?

No. You can explore stories in Crew Story or create new content.

Can I use a printed children's book?

Yes. The physical-book flow uses photos of pages. Results depend on image quality and how well the AI interprets the content.
