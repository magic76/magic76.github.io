GOOD TO KNOW / 03

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Crew Teachers and Crew Friends?

Crew Teachers focus on learning support and feedback. Crew Friends prioritize natural conversations and character-based experiences.

Can I change the language I practice?

Yes. Your interface language and the language used for speaking practice are separate settings.
